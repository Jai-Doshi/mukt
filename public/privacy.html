Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Mukt has no accounts, no servers and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What we store

The borrowings, repayments, people and settings you enter are saved in your browser's local storage on the device you use. We cannot see this data and never receive it.

Network requests

  • Exchange rates. To fill in the rate for a date, Mukt asks the public Frankfurter service (frankfurter.dev) for that day's reference rate. The request contains only the two currency codes and the date. No personal or loan data is sent.
  • Fonts. The Geist typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, which receives a standard web request from your browser.

Backups

When you export a backup or CSV, the file is saved to your device. Where it goes after that is up to you.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase all data, or clear this site's data in your browser. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

Analytics and ads

None. Mukt contains no analytics, advertising or third-party trackers.
